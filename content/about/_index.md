I’m Mark Hall, an AI engineer and full-stack developer working at
[GoEngineer](https://www.goengineer.com/). I enjoy turning ambitious ideas into
software people can inspect, understand, and trust.

My current work explores local language models, agentic systems, Model Context
Protocol tools, evaluation, and the engineering required to make AI useful
outside a demo.

I’m also the creator of
[Sir Thaddeus](https://github.com/raydeStar/sir-thaddeus), an open-source,
local-first assistant for Windows with permissioned tools, durable memory, and
evidence-driven small-model experiments.

This is my personal site. The opinions here are my own.
